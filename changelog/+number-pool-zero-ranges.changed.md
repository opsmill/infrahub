**Breaking:** a `NumberPool` attribute that declares neither `start_range` nor `end_range` now creates a pool with zero ranges, so every allocation from it fails, instead of a pool with one range from `1` to the largest supported integer. Pools of attributes that existed before the upgrade keep their range. Declare `ranges` on such attributes, see [How to migrate number pools to `ranges`](https://docs.infrahub.app/release-notes/deprecation-guides/number-pool-ranges).
