Added a `NonNegativeInt` GraphQL scalar and applied it to pagination arguments (`limit`, `offset`, `log_limit`, `log_offset`) across the branch, event, task, search, relationship, account-token, resource-manager and diff-tree queries, as well as the generated node filters. Negative or non-integer pagination values are now rejected at the schema level with a clear error that points to the offending argument, extending the resolver-level guard to every paginated query.
