Added a `NonNegativeInt` GraphQL scalar and applied it to the pagination arguments (`limit`, `offset`, `log_limit`, `log_offset`) of the branch, event, task, search, relationship, account-token, resource-manager and diff-tree queries. Negative or non-integer pagination values on these queries are now rejected at the schema level with a clear error that points to the offending argument, complementing the existing resolver-level validation on the generic node queries.
