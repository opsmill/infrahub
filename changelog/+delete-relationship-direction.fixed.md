Fixed deleting a node when a kind declares an inbound and an outbound relationship that share an identifier and can link to the same peer kind. A cascading delete now follows only the links of the relationship that cascades, instead of also deleting the peers linked through the other relationship of the pair. A delete is no longer refused when the peer holds the node only through the optional relationship of the pair, and the error now names the mandatory relationship instead of the first relationship with that identifier.
