**Breaking:** artifact generation now deletes artifacts whose target is no longer a member of the artifact definition's target group. Previously such artifacts were left behind indefinitely as stale copies, for example after merging a branch or proposed change that removed the target from the group. Any workflow that relied on reading those leftover artifacts must capture their content before the target is removed: the next full generation pass over the definition (after a merge, a definition update, or a call to the generate endpoint without a node filter) deletes them.
