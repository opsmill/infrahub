Added preferences tabs to the account settings page: a **Preferences** tab (`/profile/preferences`) where users pick their own date format (curated presets, including relative time) and timezone (searchable IANA list), with inherited organisation defaults shown as hints and a reset-to-global action; and an **Organisation defaults** tab (`/profile/organisation-defaults`) to edit the organisation-wide defaults, visible only to users with update permission on `CoreGlobalPreference`.
