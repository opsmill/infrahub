The `name` argument on the `DiffTree` and `DiffTreeSummary` GraphQL queries now retrieves the diff stored under that name. `DiffTreeSummary` previously returned a `500` whenever `name` was supplied, and `DiffTree` returned `null` for a diff whose period fell outside the default range of the branch point to the present, so a diff calculated by `DiffUpdate` for a custom period could only be retrieved by repeating its exact time range.
