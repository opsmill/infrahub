Merging or rebasing a branch now handles a kind, attribute or relationship renamed on one side while the other side changed it: previously the validation could not find the element under its old name and the operation failed with an internal error. A kind removed or an attribute renamed on the destination after the branch was created is now migrated over the rows the merge lands, instead of failing the post-merge migrations or leaving those rows in their pre-rename shape, and a kind renamed on the destination keeps its new name in the merged schema.
