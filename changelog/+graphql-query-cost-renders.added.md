Added two settings, both off by default, for the artifacts of stored GraphQL queries with the `high` cost level: `INFRAHUB_GRAPHQL_COST_LIMIT_HIGH_COST_RENDERS` runs the renders estimated above the cost limit for their target at most `INFRAHUB_GRAPHQL_COST_HIGH_COST_RENDERS_AT_ONCE` at a time (1 by default) across every task worker, in artifact checks and in artifact generation, and `INFRAHUB_GRAPHQL_COST_SKIP_HIGH_COST_ARTIFACT_CHECKS` skips those renders in the artifact checks of a proposed change, with the new `skipped` check and validator conclusion, which does not block the merge; the artifacts are generated after the merge.
