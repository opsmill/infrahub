Fixed the links between traces and logs in the Grafana data sources of the observability stack. A log line links to its trace by its `trace_id` field, in text and JSON lines, and in the `TraceID=` form that the Alloy configuration of the stack writes, with or without `0x`. A trace links to its log lines in any Loki, from one hour before to one hour after the span: the lines whose `trace_id` field contains the trace ID. The `RequestID` link is removed.
