## Executive Summary

This is the second critique. It reviews the spec and plan at commit `3a5eecb889`, after the edits
from the [first critique](critique-20261104-142632.md), the answers to its three questions, and the
change that lets the operator open the server to a network with TLS.

The two must-address items of the first critique are resolved:

- **The audit scope.** FR-022 and FR-022a now cover every log stream in Loki, the metric labels and
  the trace resource attributes.
- **The port.** FR-006 keeps the loopback address as the default, and FR-006a adds HTTPS served by
  `mcp-grafana` itself.

The read-only 1.13 release (FR-010, FR-017a) removes the largest security risk. No item blocks
`/speckit-tasks`.

The new findings come mostly from the network and TLS change:

- Opening the port to a network without TLS is allowed and only discouraged in the docs. The reason
  given in research R3 ("the entrypoint does not see the host address") does not hold, because
  Compose can pass the same variable into the container.
- Helm can refuse a `LoadBalancer` or `NodePort` Service without TLS, but the contract leaves it to the
  docs.
- A few texts in the spec and plan still describe the state before the TLS change or before the
  critique.
